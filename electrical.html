Residential electrical

Wiring that keeps up with how you actually live

Our electrical services are a standout, featuring everything from comprehensive system upgrades to efficient lighting installations and repairs.

  1. 01

    Electrical repairs

    Troubleshooting and repair of faults in existing wiring, outlets, switches, fixtures and circuits — including the dead circuits, tripping breakers and flickering lighting that send most people looking for an electrician.

  2. 02

    Whole house rewires

    Replacement of outdated or unsafe wiring throughout the house, bringing circuits, grounding and outlets up to current code — common in older Pasadena, Altadena and Highland Park homes.

  3. 03

    Panel upgrade

    Upgrade the electrical panel so it can carry modern loads: EV charging, air conditioning, kitchen appliances and additions. Panel upgrades may qualify for Charge Ready Home rebates.

  4. 04

    Lighting installation

    Efficient lighting installations throughout the home, from recessed and kitchen lighting to exterior and outdoor-space fixtures.

  5. 05

    EV charger installation

    Equip your home for the future with our EV charger installation service, ensuring your electric vehicles are always ready to go.

  6. 06

    Electrical for remodels and ADUs

    Because we also build, the electrical for a remodel, home addition or ADU is planned and installed by the same company handling the construction.

Commercial & industrial electrical

Power your building can rely on

We offer comprehensive electrical solutions for commercial properties, including wiring, lighting installations, and electrical system maintenance to ensure reliability and compliance with all safety standards.

  1. 01

    Switchgear installation

    Expert installation of switchgear systems to manage electrical loads and enhance the safety and functionality of your electrical distribution system.

  2. 02

    Solar panel installation

    Equip your commercial properties with solar panels to reduce energy costs and promote sustainability. Our services include planning, installation, and maintenance of high-efficiency solar solutions. There are incentives to install solar in California — ask us how they apply to your property.

  3. 03

    Commercial wiring & maintenance

    Wiring, lighting installations and electrical system maintenance that keep commercial properties reliable and compliant with all safety standards.

  4. 04

    Tenant improvement electrical

    Electrical alterations that go with tenant improvement work, upgrading or altering commercial spaces to tenant specifications and business needs.

  5. 05

    Heavy machinery connections

    Installation of heavy machinery with the supply and connections properly set up and integrated into existing systems for optimal performance.

  6. 06

    Safety and compliance upgrades

    Targeted electrical upgrades and compliance checks that help facilities meet safety and regulatory requirements, minimizing risks and enhancing workplace safety.
