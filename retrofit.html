Electrical panel rebates

Up to $4,200 toward an electrical panel upgrade

Southern California Edison’s Charge Ready Home Program is offering up to $4,200 in rebates for electrical panel upgrades to prepare your home for EV charger installation. If your retrofit involves the panel, it is worth checking whether the rebate applies.

Our expert team will guide you every step of the way and ensure you maximize your rebate benefits.
